The EPL in 10,000 different universes

Every fan asks the same thing after a weekend: can we still do it? The table can't answer that. So after every Gameweek I play the rest of the Premier League season 10,000 times and count where your club ends up.

How this works, and where it is weak

Method

1. Every club gets a strength rating, updated after every league match since 2010.

2. The rating gap between two clubs sets how many goals each is expected to score. Home advantage is included.

3. Every remaining match is played with random goals around those expectations. That gives one possible season.

4. Repeat 10,000 times, apply the real league rules for ties, and count.

Limits

It knows results only. It does not know about injuries, transfers, managers or fixture congestion.

It predicts slightly fewer goals than are really scored.

Two runs on the same data differ by about 1 point. A change under 3 points is noise, not news.

I support Liverpool. The model does not know that.
